How customer types are decided

Repeat

4 or more visits, and visited in the last 60 days

Occasional

2 to 3 visits, and visited in the last 60 days

Rare

Only 1 visit, more than 30 days ago

New

First visit in the last 30 days

Inactive

No visit for more than 60 days

These rules can be changed to match how you want to define your customers.
